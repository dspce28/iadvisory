Your CIBIL Score

What the number means, what moves it, and what to do before you apply.

What the bands mean

750 – 900 — Excellent

Best rates and the widest choice of lenders.

700 – 749 — Good

Approved by most lenders, pricing slightly above best.

650 – 699 — Fair

Approvals happen, but expect a higher rate or smaller sanction.

550 – 649 — Poor

Limited options. Worth repairing before applying.

Below 550 — Very poor

Focus on repair first; applying now mostly costs you enquiries.

What drives the number

~35%
Payment history

Whether you pay on time, every time. The heaviest single factor.

~30%
Credit utilisation

How much of your available limit you use. Stay under 30%.

~15%
Length of history

How long your accounts have been open. Keep your oldest card.

~10%
Credit mix

A balance of secured and unsecured borrowing reads better than one type.

~10%
Recent enquiries

Several applications in a short window reads as credit hunger.

Weightings are approximate and CIBIL does not publish an exact formula. The ordering, however, is consistent: payment history and utilisation dominate.
